Start here · straight from the UN

The real SDG icons, logo and color wheel.

The UN gives out the official SDG icons, the SDG logo and color wheel, posters, and a pack for each goal with social media cards, a GIF and an infographic. Everything comes in all six UN languages, and anyone can use the icons and color wheel by following the UN’s guidelines.

Open the UN’s download page ↗ 17 icons for web (.zip) Logo and wheel (.zip) Usage guidelines (PDF)

Your goal’s pack

Social media cards, a GIF and an infographic for one goal. Each tile downloads a .zip from un.org.

Do

  • Use the icons and color wheel to show which goal your app is about.
  • Use the versions without the UN emblem. The emblem versions are only for UN organizations.
  • Link to un.org/sustainabledevelopment and add the UN’s disclaimer line (below) to your app.

Don’t

  • Make it look like the UN made, approved or sponsors your app.
  • Build your own logo out of the wheel or the icons.
  • Use them to sell something or raise money. That needs the UN’s written permission.

Paste this at the bottom of your app if you use the icons

<p>The content of this publication has not been approved by the United Nations and does not reflect the views of the United Nations or its officials or Member States. <a href="https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment">un.org/sustainabledevelopment</a></p>

Rules summarized from the UN’s communications materials page and FAQ, checked October 12, 2026. Read the guidelines if you’re unsure.

  • Two fonts, max.One with personality for headings, one that’s easy to read for everything else.
  • One accent color.Use it for the thing you want people to notice. Your SDG’s color is a great pick.
  • Motion should mean something.Data arrived, you got it right, something changed. Wiggling for no reason just distracts.
  • “One HTML file” vs “Needs React”.One-file tools work in Gemini Canvas and GitHub Pages with a single line. React tools need a React project.
Built in

The official SDG colors.

Each goal has its own color. Use your goal’s color as your accent and your app instantly looks connected to it. Click one to copy its hex code.

Glow-up prompts

Make it look designed, one prompt at a time.

Once your app works, send these one by one. Check the result after each one.

  1. FontsUse the Google Fonts "Bricolage Grotesque" for headings and "Inter" for everything else, with a warm off-white background (#F6F3EA) and generous spacing.
  2. ColorPick one accent color and use it everywhere it matters: the official SDG 13 green, #3F7E44.
  3. LoadingWhile the data loads, show grey placeholder boxes in the shape of the content instead of a blank screen.
  4. MotionWhen the data arrives, animate the cards in one after another with GSAP from the jsDelivr CDN. Keep it under one second, and skip it for people who turned on “reduce motion”.
  5. PhoneMake it look great on a phone first, then on a laptop. Buttons should be big enough to tap.
