    Your job today
    Don’t try to fix global warming with a website. Pick one tiny, specific sliver of a global problem, grab a live public dataset, and build a tool that makes a real person go, “Huh, that actually makes sense to me now.”
    1. Find the annoyancePick one sliver of a real problem that bugs you.
    2. Grab live dataChoose a free API from our list. No keys, no signup.
    3. Pitch it in 3 sentencesNo pitch, no prompt. That’s the rule.
    4. Brief your AI internPaste a starter prompt, then improve it one step at a time.
    5. Ship it and demoPut it online and show a live prototype.
    Four questions to steal

    Start with a question you actually want answered.

    01 · EARTH RIGHT NOW

    How many earthquakes hit Earth while you were asleep last night?

    Usually dozens. You felt none of them. Some of them hit cities.

    The twistMake it feel like a text from planet Earth, not a science report: “While you slept, Earth shook 82 times.” One sentence first, the map second.

          Or find your own
          
    Start with what’s annoying, not with a goal.
    
          
    Picking “Zero Hunger” and staring at it doesn’t work. Look for things that are broken, clunky or confusing instead. Real products start there.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            
    The annoyance scale
    
            
    The world is full of terrible software and clunky systems. What’s a piece of information that is hard to find, hard to understand, or locked away, and should be three clicks away for a normal human being?
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Reverse-engineer an app you love
    
            
    TikTok, Duolingo and Spotify don’t work because their databases are cool. They work because they fix a human friction point. What friction does your favorite app fix?
    
            
    Now flip it. We have tons of climate data. The problem is that it’s boring and nobody looks at it. What would the Duolingo of climate data look like?

          How to work with AI
          
    Your AI is an expensive, lazy intern.
    
        
    
        
    
          It knows every coding language perfectly, but it has zero common sense, zero creativity and no empathy. Tell it to “build a green app” and you’ll get something incredibly boring. You are the architect. The AI is just holding the hammer.
        
    
        
      
          
    • Be specific.Say who it’s for, what it shows and how it should feel. Vague in, boring out.
      • 
          
    • One change per message.Small steps are easy to check and easy to undo.
      • 
          
    • Paste the exact error.Copy the red text from the browser console and say what you expected to happen.
      • 
          
    • Make it explain.Ask “what does this part do?” until you could explain it to a friend.
      • 
          
    • Check its work.AI code has bugs and security holes. Click every button. Try weird inputs.
      • 
          
    • Never paste secrets.No passwords, no API keys, no personal info. Everything on our data list is keyless anyway.
      • 
        
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    
        
    
          How you win
          
    “Does it work?” is not the question. “Does it matter?” is.
    
          
    At the end you’ll show a live prototype, not a code file. Nobody is grading your syntax. We’re going to look at the screen and ask one thing:
    
        
    
        
    “If I stumbled across this on the internet, would I actually use it for more than 5 seconds?”
